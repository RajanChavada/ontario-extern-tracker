What is a clinical extern in Ontario?

Short answer: a clinical extern is a nursing (or other health) student hired by a hospital as a paid unregulated care provider. Externs work under the supervision of regulated staff, outside their school curriculum. It is not a clinical placement, and it is not the Nursing Graduate Guarantee.

Who runs the extern program?

Hospitals hire externs through Ontario's Enhanced Extern Program, which offers clinical learners paid hospital work. Ontario Health says students are matched with hospitals twice a year, once in October and again in February. In practice, each hospital still posts its own job and sets its own criteria.

Source: Ontario Health: Enhanced Extern Program

When do Ontario hospitals recruit externs?

There's no single date. Examples from hospitals' own pages (checked Nov 23, 2026):

  • CHEO (Ottawa): postings usually go up in February/March for a spring cohort around May. That's their typical window, not a guaranteed opening. source
  • SickKids: the 2026 posting was live for one week (Jan 19–25, 2026). source
  • LHSC (London): intakes run regularly through the year, with start dates in February, May, June and July. source
  • UHN: nursing extern start dates in February, June, August and October. source

Because some windows are short, a calendar reminder for each closing date helps. The tracker can add one for you.

"Open" doesn't always mean a vacancy

Some extern postings are talent pools, collecting applications for future openings. For example, Mackenzie Health's posting says it "is not for an existing vacancy", and SHN's 2026 posting is titled "Talent Pool". Others are for real vacancies: each of Humber River Health's extern postings says it's posted for an existing vacancy. It's worth knowing which one you're applying to. Typical requirements (they differ by hospital)

  • Year of study: ranges from any year (Trillium Health Partners, with preference to years 3 and 4) to year 2+ (Sunnybrook, Humber River, Michael Garron) to year 3+ (SHN, CHEO). PN students often need to be in their final year.
  • BCLS: several postings require a current Basic Cardiac Life Support certificate (SHN, Mackenzie, Sunnybrook).
  • Academic standing: postings say "good" or "excellent" academic standing. None we checked states a number.
  • Availability: e.g. UHN's last extern posting asked for at least 18.75 hours a week.

Always read the posting itself, because criteria change between postings.

FAQ

Is a clinical extern paid? Yes. Externs are hired as employees. Rates on open postings (checked Nov 23, 2026) run from $17.60/hr (Humber River) to $26.756/hr (Trillium Health Partners), with Michael Garron at $20.60, Mackenzie at $22.00 and SHN at $25.379.

Can first-year students apply? Sometimes. Trillium Health Partners' posting accepts nursing students in any year of study, with preference to years 3 and 4. Most others start at year 2 or 3, and Mackenzie Health lists students entering year 2, so check how your year counts.

Is the NGG a guaranteed job? No. Employers request funding per new nurse, positions aren't funded on units with nursing reductions, and you can apply only once.
